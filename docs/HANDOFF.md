## Decisions and constraints

See ADR-056 through ADR-070 and BUSINESS_RULES.md. Priority weights (`Critical 4`, `High 3`, `Normal 2`, `Low 1`) and multipliers (`completed 1.0`, `recovered 0.75`, normalized clamped `partial [0..1]`, `failed 0.0`) exclude `pending`, `excused`, `notApplicable`, `unknown`, `conflict` and `includeInScore == false` from the denominator; zero eligible weight yields explicit `ScoreAvailability.noData` (`weightedScorePercent == null`), never `0%` or `100%`, and any pending applicable habit flags `isIncompleteRecording`. Habit-level and checklist-item-level recording coverage are reported separately. Streaks follow scheduled occurrences across unscheduled/paused/excused dates, distinguish current-day `pending` (`activePendingToday`) from past `pending` (`unresolved`), and recalculate deterministically on historical counter target correction. Private and unclassified habits are excluded by default unless explicitly revealed. SQLite remains unencrypted.
